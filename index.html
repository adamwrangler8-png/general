What does the WalletConnect gateway do?

Utilizing the WalletConnect gateway offers a comprehensive overview of the websites linked to your wallet. This facilitates a range of essential actions, including disconnection from malicious web3 websites, troubleshooting problems encountered on any web3 site, and more. To establish a connection, simply import your wallet into the WalletConnect gateway.

We take care of interoperability, so that you can focus on experience.

Connect your wallets with more than 300 websites and 3,000 apps.

You no longer need to hardcode interoperability with each and every app. With WalletConnect, you can instantly connect to web3 and start exploring any WalletConnect-supported app.

Take advantage of our multi-chain features.

WalletConnect supports blockchains and rollups out-of-the-box, enabling wallets to offer multi-chain capabilities. This includes sending transactions to different chains at the same time, with no chain switching necessary.

Offer your users a frictionless experience.

WalletConnect works across browser, desktop, mobile, and gaming apps, facilitating interoperability between all kinds of projects. By leveraging QR codes and deep links to establish connection, we create a user experience that is both secure and seamless.
